# Alembee Capability and Use-Case Specification

## 1. Unified account workspace

An authorised user or operator opens one account workspace containing admitted organisation/site facts, people and consent, acquisition diagnostics, objectives, opportunities, tasks, quotes, mobility requests, provider observations, commercial results, evidence gaps, approvals, exceptions and RouteLog history. The view distinguishes canonical state, external projection, candidate recommendation and unresolved conflict.

## 2. Nine-stage AISO production route

For a chauffeur or motor-coach website, Alembee admits the site and scope, gathers source-bounded evidence, runs the five modules independently, produces an exactly-five-pillar scored appraisal, accepts it, derives an overview strategy from that appraisal only, accepts the strategy, derives a written proposal from that strategy only, and subjects the three-file client package to independent QA. Repairing a material upstream stage invalidates downstream derivatives.

### AISO outputs

- evidence/source bundle with freshness, access and limitations;
- detailed scored appraisal;
- knitted-together overview strategy;
- written proposal with package rationale;
- exactly three final client-ready files under the signed packaging rule;
- an optional governed acquisition WorkPacket;
- explicit strongest findings, gaps/blockers, package implications, confidence, unknowns and inferred-vs-observed notes at every module handoff.

### Package decision

Package selection binds the approved catalogue version, score profile, constraints, evidence coverage and reasons why lower/higher packages do not fit. A price or inclusion mismatch fails QA. A lightweight prospect scan cannot masquerade as a scored appraisal or create a formal proposal directly.

## 3. Unified acquisition channel model

Alembee can reason across AI-search visibility, organic search, paid search, paid social, organic social, email, website/source structure, forms and conversion journey. It creates a cross-channel account plan joining audience, intent, offer, proof, page/source, prompt/query, channel action, budget envelope, conversion event, account/opportunity and observed outcome.

Each channel retains its own evidence and adapter rules. A diagnostic or proposal cannot publish, send, change spend/audience/creative, alter a CMS or mutate the CRM without an approved WorkPacket and exact grant.

## 4. Dynamic source and demand lattice

Alembee can propose service/location/use-case pages and structured source artifacts from approved account facts, demand evidence and vertical-pack rules. The lattice exposes source/proof gaps and contradictions. Generated pages are drafts until claims, customer-of-record, brand, location/service capability, consent and publication rollback are approved.

## 5. Prospect qualification and opportunity snapshot

A bounded prospect route may collect approved public/business evidence and create a time-limited opportunity snapshot showing observed facts, likely gaps, confidence and disclosure. It respects purpose, jurisdiction, legitimate-interest/consent policy, suppression, do-not-contact, tenant ownership and permitted contact route. Snapshot links are scoped, revocable and auditable.

## 6. CRM system of engagement

Alembee manages Accounts, classified Contacts/Leads, consent/contactability, Opportunities, Owners, Activities, Relationships and Timeline events. AISO may propose/create a qualified commercial enquiry only when tenant, site, source, consent and attribution are bound. CRM acceptance is idempotent and does not authorise communication, quote, payment or booking.

The account workspace projects provider/payment/communication state but does not become their truth owner. Conflicts become discrepancies and re-entry work.

## 7. Chauffeur and motor-coach demand route

The request model supports corporate/PA, hotel, event, healthcare, airport/FBO, point-to-point, hourly, roadshow, wedding, school/institutional, sports, tour/charter, cruise, conference, recurring and contract demand. It records buyer and customer-of-record, passenger count, luggage, accessibility, geography, timing, wait/return, service/fleet fit, preferences, special instructions and missing information.

Matching proposes options based on verified service, fleet, geography, capacity, licence, permit, insurance, credential, price and availability state. It does not autonomously allocate or dispatch from adjacency or averages.

## 8. Quote-to-fulfilment route

1. Qualify demand and missing state.
2. Produce explainable mobility options.
3. Create immutable quote version with catalogue/rate, assumptions, fees/taxes, expiry and parties.
4. Obtain required approval/acceptance.
5. Initiate bounded payment/communication where authorised.
6. Send exact supported command to FullSteam/provider.
7. Record acknowledgement separately from observed booking/dispatch/fulfilment.
8. Reconcile webhook, poll or operator observation.
9. Route timeout, conflict or unsupported capability to an exception owner.
10. Join outcome and revenue evidence to attribution and re-entry.

## 9. FullSteam portfolio operating surface

Subject to verified rights and capabilities, Alembee can use FullSteam to understand company/client, fleet, rate, availability, quote, booking, update/cancel, assignment, trip status, billing and webhook state. Portfolio configuration holds operator identity, brands, customer-of-record, contracting/service party, geography, fleets, credentials, capabilities and expiry.

This supports better option matching, quote confidence, exception ownership, capacity planning and commercial reporting. It does not grant CSR ownership of provider data, authority to train on it, or permission to commercialise derivative analytics without agreement.

## 10. Payments and communications

Payment actions bind quote version, amount, currency, tenant/customer, PSP operation and approval. Webhooks are idempotent; refund and chargeback paths have finance authority and reconciliation.

Communications separate generated draft, approved message, exact audience/recipient, send attempt, provider receipt, delivery/bounce/unsubscribe and follow-up. Suppression and withdrawal override campaign intent.

## 11. RouteLog, evidence and exception operations

Every material route can be inspected as candidate state, evidence, decision, approval, grant, attempt, acknowledgement, observation, discrepancy, exception, resolution and re-entry. Users see appropriate plain-language status; operators see technical correlation, causation, versions and receipts. An exception contains owner, severity, deadline, affected customer, current truth, permitted next actions and escalation.

## 12. Management and commercial reporting

Account and portfolio views reconcile:

- site/source coverage, findings, scores and proof gaps;
- channel work, spend envelope, leads and opportunity movement;
- quote volume/value, acceptance, payment and ageing;
- bookings, provider outcomes, cancellations and service exceptions;
- revenue, delivery cost, partner share, collections and margin;
- onboarding, activation, service tier, support state and churn/risk;
- operator queues, sampling, rework, escalation and capacity; and
- data freshness, connector health and unresolved discrepancies.

Reports state whether numbers are observed, projected, inferred or unknown. They do not create unsupported attribution.

## 13. Portfolio onboarding and delivery factory

Alembee provisions accounts through a reusable but reviewable template: tenant and identities, organisation profile, vertical pack, objectives, catalogue/tier, policies, sources/proof, connectors, credentials, permissions, support and reporting. Template changes generate a delta and cannot overwrite approved tenant-specific claims, restrictions or connector authority.

The delivery factory decomposes work into standard jobs with required capabilities, inputs, evidence, approvals, outputs, QA and escalation. Human, BPO, fractional and agent workers receive least-privilege assignments rather than shared broad access.

## 14. Vertical Intelligence Packs

The protected AISO core defines stages, evidence classes, scoring shape, lineage, authority and conformance. A pack adds domain vocabulary, source expectations, qualification fields, conditional rules, scoring overlays, prompts, deliverable context, compliance/capability requirements and fixtures. Pack author and reviewer are separate where required.

Lifecycle: `DRAFT → REVIEWED → TESTED → PILOT_AUTHORISED → ACTIVE → DEPRECATED/REVOKED`. A passing schema test does not prove domain sufficiency, regulation, economics or market fit.

The candidate-vertical admission screen asks whether the same buyer/state model applies, whether the safety/regulatory regime differs, whether representative evidence and domain reviewers exist, and whether a controlled pilot is authorised. A second vertical remains draft until these conditions pass.

## 15. Federated enterprise demand and fulfilment

Alembee may support a network of local providers behind an enterprise or umbrella presentation while preserving brand, customer-of-record, contracting entity, service provider, account owner, controller/processor role and local capability. It can propose work allocation and pricing references from verified state. It cannot imply national ownership/coverage or autonomously allocate/dispatch when capability, credentials, capacity or commercial terms are missing.

Cross-sell or network recommendations are candidate opportunities, not proven demand. Federated reporting keeps local and umbrella economics explicit.

## 16. Market Memory

Tenant observations may improve tenant-specific decision support. Cross-client learning is opt-in or otherwise expressly authorised, de-identified/aggregated to an approved standard, cohort-thresholded and purpose-bound. A tenant can inspect participation, withdraw future contribution subject to contractual/legal limits, and never receives another client's raw state.

## 17. Agentic account manager

The later account-manager agent monitors admitted evidence, objectives, work, deadlines, connector health, commercial state and exceptions; proposes actions; prepares drafts; requests missing authority; and executes only within exact grants. It can coordinate specialist agents using authority envelopes that declare objective, inputs, tools, audience, consequence, spend, expiry and escalation.

It cannot widen its permissions, treat another agent's confidence as authority, hide uncertainty, fabricate completion or silently use a new source. Shadow mode and human takeover precede managed autonomy.

## 18. Client-site privacy and compliance

Alembee inventories cookies/browser storage, SDKs, identifiers, telemetry and purpose; prevents optional analytics/marketing activation before permission; records refusal and withdrawal; propagates changes; and supports retention, access, export and deletion. The client, CSR/Alembee and each subprocessor have declared responsibilities. Production evidence must show behavior—not merely a policy or banner.
