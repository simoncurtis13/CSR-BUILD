# CSR/Alembee Open Object Register

These items do not block approval of the planning baseline. They block the named implementation or release step until resolved with evidence.

| ID | Open object | Blocks | Required evidence | Accountable owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O-01 | Executed source-use, derivative and asset schedule for every donor | Creation or production use of CSR derivatives | Signed IPBoundarySchedule/SourceLicence covering code, data, brands, improvements, SaaS, support and exit | Commercial/legal owner |
| O-02 | Complete current repository pins and provenance for every GPTO line | AISO source selection and migration | URLs, SHAs, inventories, build/tests and relationship decision | Technical lead |
| O-03 | FAR file-level map and current test/staging state | FAR source reuse | RETAIN/REMODEL/REMOVE/ADD/VERIFY map and reproducible checks | FAR/CSR technical owners |
| O-04 | cdHQ ATS data disposition | CRM migration | Purpose, lawful basis, tenant mapping, retention/archive/delete rules and rehearsal receipts | Data owner |
| O-05 | FullSteam capabilities and commercial/data rights | Live provider actions and commercial claims | Capability discovery, sandbox observations, API/right matrix, SLA/freshness, controller/processor and derivative-data terms | Provider/commercial owner |
| O-06 | CSR-controlled infrastructure and account independence | Customer staging/production | Repo/cloud/domain/database/model/billing/secrets/comms/monitoring ownership inventory | Technical/security owners |
| O-07 | GIA production switch criteria | GIA-labelled admission | Conformance pack, divergence report, tenancy, persistence, replay and operational evidence | GIA owner |
| O-08 | CIG production switch criteria | CIG-labelled transition completion | v0.2 conformance, route-law fixtures, persistence/replay and operational evidence | CIG owner |
| O-09 | Patent status, ownership, scope and freedom to operate | Patent claims and protected commercial assertions | Qualified legal advice and ownership/filing records | Legal owner |
| O-10 | Consent/legitimate-interest and outbound policy by jurisdiction | Live prospecting and communications | Approved policy, suppression/unsubscribe behavior and tests | Privacy/commercial owner |
| O-11 | Payment/refund/chargeback operating authority | Live payment operations | PSP configuration, approval matrix, reconciliation and finance procedures | Finance owner |
| O-12 | Initial pilot account and authorised data | Controlled end-to-end validation | Signed pilot scope, contacts, consent, credentials, provider sandbox and success criteria | Product/commercial owner |
| O-13 | Portfolio capacity and support envelope | Expansion beyond pilot | Measured queue, cost, service, exception and support limits | Operations owner |
| O-14 | Second vertical selection and domain evidence | Multi-vertical claims | Independent domain review, fixtures, pack and controlled-pilot authority | Product owner |

An item closes only when its evidence URL, version/date, reviewer and decision are recorded. Verbal assurance or repository access alone does not close it.
