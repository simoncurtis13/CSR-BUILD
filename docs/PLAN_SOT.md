## 1. Purpose

This document converts the Curtis Doctrine corpus and the connected repository estate into the controlling repository plan for Alembee by CSR. `CSR-BUILD` owns cross-repository build control, traceability, delivery gates and evidence coordination. It governs what is to be built, how the existing assets are to be transformed, where authority lives, and what evidence is required before any capability may be called complete.

It does not claim that Alembee, AISO, the CRM transformation, the FullSteam integration, the CGS derivative, GIA, CIG, or the end-to-end route is implemented or production-ready. Repository presence, prose, screenshots, HTTP success, generated output and developer assertion are evidence inputs—not proof of completion.

## 2. Product definition

Alembee is one governed commercial operating system for acquiring, qualifying, converting, fulfilling and learning from customer demand. It joins:

- AISO acquisition intelligence across AI search, organic search, paid search, paid and organic social, email and governed web/source changes;
- an account, contact, consent, opportunity, activity and relationship CRM transformed from the cdHQ donor platform;
- structured passenger-mobility intake, optioning, quoting, payment, communications and provider fulfilment;
- FAR-derived quote, orchestration, telemetry and commercial components;
- verified FullSteam capabilities through a provider adapter with acknowledgement, observation, reconciliation and exception ownership;
- a CSR-controlled, authorised derivative of the Conversion CGS substrate for canonical state, governance, bounded execution, ledger, replay, tenancy and evidence;
- GIA as the exact-use admissibility seam when its standalone runtime passes its own conformance gates;
- CIG as the governed transition boundary when its standalone runtime passes its own conformance gates;
- one RouteLog and management view joining acquisition touch, commercial state, consequence, observation, attribution and re-entry.

The customer sees one product, one account, one interface, one assistant, one work queue and one evidence history. GPTO, AISO, cdHQ and FAR are source, capability or migration names rather than separate customer products.

## 3. Locked outcomes

### 3.1 First controlled release

One CSR-operated chauffeur or motor-coach account must be able to:

1. admit a tenant-bound website and evidence scope;
2. execute the locked nine-stage AISO chain;
3. complete five independent modules and an exactly-five-pillar scored appraisal;
4. derive the overview strategy only from the accepted appraisal;
5. derive the written proposal only from the accepted strategy;
6. return exactly three versioned, client-ready files and pass independent final QA;
7. create a governed acquisition WorkPacket when authorised;
8. qualify the account, buyer and passenger or enterprise demand in the CRM;
9. create and approve a versioned quote and permitted communication/payment action;
10. hand off only supported, bound operations to FullSteam or another declared provider;
11. distinguish provider acknowledgement from observed outcome;
12. reconcile unknowns, discrepancies, retries and exceptions;
13. connect outcome and revenue evidence back to source, account, action and responsible owner; and
14. preserve tenant-bound state, decision, grant, attempt, observation, exception and replay evidence at every material transition.

### 3.2 Later product modes

The same governed runtime progresses through separately evidenced modes:

1. **CSR-operated evidence:** CSR staff operate the system for bounded accounts.
2. **Customer SaaS:** customers receive guided onboarding, connectors, policies, workspaces, reports, support, billing, export and deletion.
3. **Agent shadow:** the account-manager agent proposes and observes without independent consequential authority.
4. **Agentic managed service:** a persistent account-manager agent becomes the primary interface inside tenant-, purpose-, capability-, spend- and expiry-bounded authority. Humans own exceptions and high-consequence decisions.

Passing one mode does not imply the next.

## 7. WorkPacket and route law

Every consequential route must carry:

- tenant and environment;
- actor or service identity;
- purpose, scope and consequence class;
- source and target object identities and versions;
- correlation and causation IDs;
- schema and policy versions;
- payload digest and idempotency key;
- evidence references and explicit unknowns;
- requested capability and exact operation;
- approval requirements and expiry;
- observation contract, receipt requirements and discrepancy owner;
- rollback or compensation route; and
- re-entry instruction.

The normal route is: candidate state → evidence and qualification → admissibility → transition/compute decision → exact grant → bound adapter attempt → acknowledgement → observation/reconciliation → canonical state update → attribution → re-entry. Any bypass fails closed or becomes a non-consequential proposal.

## 8. AISO contract

AISO must preserve the SRC-079 source order and lineage:

1. admitted site and evidence scope;
2. module evidence collection;
3. five independent module handoffs;
4. exactly-five-pillar scored appraisal;
5. accepted appraisal;
6. overview strategy generated solely from that accepted appraisal;
7. accepted strategy;
8. written proposal generated solely from that accepted strategy; and
9. independent final QA and exactly-three-file package.

The five pillars are:

1. Intent Intelligence and Content Planning;
2. Technical Readiness;
3. AI and Search Signal Clarity;
4. Growth Readiness and Conversion Architecture; and
5. Content Authority and Trust Signals.

Every finding is `OBSERVED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `INFERRED` or `UNKNOWN`, with source, timestamp/freshness, confidence and limitation. AISO never converts absent analytics, search-console, Core Web Vitals, booking, CRM or revenue access into a negative observation. It never guarantees rankings, AI placement, leads, bookings, conversion, ROI, revenue or operational capacity.

Chauffeur and motor-coach interpretation lives in a versioned Vertical Intelligence Pack bound to the protected core, organisation profile, rubric, prompts and catalogue. A pack cannot add a sixth pillar, skip a stage, weaken evidence classes, alter authority, import recruitment semantics or execute an action.

## 9. CRM transformation contract

`Alembee-cdhq` is a substantial ATS and lead-marketplace donor, not a completed CSR CRM. Transformation is explicit and history-preserving:

- company/client → Account;
- person/candidate → classified Contact or Lead only with purpose and lawful basis;
- recruiter/owner → AccountOwner or WorkOwner;
- application pipeline → approved Lead/Opportunity stages;
- activity/interview → Task, Meeting or TimelineEvent where appropriate;
- source → AcquisitionAttribution with preserved provenance.

No blanket label or table rename is permitted. Candidate records, CVs, recruitment documents, interviews and recruitment-only history stay outside the live CRM unless purpose, lawful basis, tenant, retention, mapping and reconciliation are approved. Recruitment routes, roles, permissions, reports, seed data and navigation must become unreachable in the CSR release. Archive and deletion actions require receipts and rollback rules.

## 10. Mobility, quote and provider contract

Mobility intake supports chauffeur, airport/FBO, point-to-point, hourly, roadshow, wedding, event shuttle, school/institutional, sports, tour/charter, cruise, conference, recurring and contract demand, with sedan, SUV, sprinter/executive van, minibus and motor-coach fit. Buyer, geography, passenger, luggage, return/wait, accessibility, special instruction, fleet/capacity, licensing and credential state are explicit.

Quotes are versioned, explainable and bound to customer-of-record, presentation brand, contracting entity, service provider, currency, taxes/fees, assumptions, expiry and acceptance state. A changed payload invalidates the prior approval.

The FullSteam adapter begins from capability discovery. Each function—client, fleet, rate, availability, quote, booking create/update/cancel, assignment, trip status, billing, webhook and receipt—is `SUPPORTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `UNVERIFIED` or `RESTRICTED`. No function is marketed or executed until API right, scope, sandbox behavior, freshness and ownership are verified.

## 12. Commercial and operating model

The runtime must support:

- CSR-operated delivery, customer SaaS and later governed agentic management;
- service tier, catalogue, price, inclusion, margin, capacity and support configuration;
- account onboarding, connector and credential setup, source/proof hydration and policy configuration;
- customer-of-record, contracting entity, provider, account owner and controller/processor roles;
- partner, affiliate, owned and umbrella-brand distinctions without implied ownership or coverage;
- BPO/fractional operator assignment with least privilege, sampling, two-person review where required and escalation;
- account and portfolio reporting for acquisition, leads, quotes, bookings, revenue, delivery cost, partner share, collections, margin, support, exceptions and risk;
- tenant-controlled Market Memory participation, cohort thresholds and no default cross-client leakage; and
- demand/fulfilment proposals that remain proposals until capacity, credentials, terms and authority are established.

## 13. Security and governance invariants

- Tenant and service identity are mandatory for every privileged route.
- Cross-tenant read, write, replay, hydration and connector access fail closed.
- A grant binds actor, tenant, purpose, operation, target, payload hash, environment, capability, expiry and policy version.
- Agents cannot widen their own authority, spend or tool set.
- Secrets are never committed, logged, embedded in prompts or copied across tenants.
- Prompt injection, untrusted source content and generated artifacts cannot change policy or capability.
- Telemetry and model output remain evidence candidates until admitted.
- Replay is simulation-only at any production adapter boundary.
- Duplicate requests and webhooks are idempotent and recorded.
- Timeout after attempt produces `UNKNOWN` plus reconciliation—not optimistic success.
- Every exception has a named owner, deadline, customer-visible state where appropriate and re-entry path.

## 15. Delivery sequence

The target remains an eight-week controlled-staging envelope after PD0. Weekly boundaries are evidence gates:

- **PD0:** repository plans, cross-repository handover, owners, dependencies, migrations, acceptance tests and rollback approved.
- **W0:** freeze sources; reproduce builds/tests; complete rights, independence, data and capability inventories.
- **W1:** establish CSR-controlled environments, identity, CI, canonical object contracts and security repairs.
- **W2:** implement WorkPacket, RouteLog, transition/compute decisions, grants, receipts and replay-safe boundaries.
- **W3:** create CRM schemas and migration tooling; remove ATS reachability in controlled builds.
- **W4:** close the nine-stage AISO pipeline, evidence schema, five-pillar scoring and three-file QA.
- **W5:** integrate mobility intake, versioned quote, payment and communications in sandbox/shadow mode.
- **W6:** integrate verified FullSteam capabilities with observation, reconciliation and exception UI.
- **W7:** complete attribution/re-entry, management reporting, compliance controls and end-to-end negative tests.
- **W8:** rehearse migration and rollback, run controlled cohort/portfolio simulation, security/load review and signed release decision.

No live adapter consequence ships ahead of identity, tenant, grant, receipt and reconciliation paths.

## 16. Completion standard

Planning is complete when every requirement has a repository owner, target component, dependency, migration, acceptance test, negative test, evidence location and rollback path, and the cross-repository handover is approved.

Implementation is complete only when the controlled release route in section 3 passes on named commits and environments with reproducible build/test logs, migration receipts, security evidence, adapter observations, reconciliation and signed release approval. A dashboard, report, API response, build plan or generated document alone is insufficient.

## 17. Change control

Any change to the locked product, AISO chain, first vertical, repository boundary, authority model, evidence classes, customer-of-record rules or release gate requires a change record containing source, reason, affected contracts, data/security impact, migration impact, acceptance-test changes, rollback and approving authority.
