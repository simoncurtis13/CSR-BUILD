# Alembee by CSR — Product Constitution

**Status:** Foundational doctrine  
**Applies to:** Alembee product, AISO, CRM, mobility, provider integrations, operators and agents

## Mission

Alembee turns customer acquisition and service fulfilment into one governed, observable commercial route. It helps an organisation find demand, understand it, act within authority, deliver through the right people and systems, and learn from what actually happened.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **One customer product.** Customers use Alembee, not a federation of donor brands and dashboards.
2. **Evidence before assertion.** Unknown, unverified, inferred, attempted and observed remain distinct.
3. **Proposal is not authority.** AI output, a score or an operator recommendation cannot execute itself.
4. **Exact bounded action.** Consequences require tenant, identity, purpose, scope, target, payload, capability, policy and expiry binding.
5. **Provider truth stays with the provider.** Internal projections never overwrite contradictory external observations.
6. **Acknowledgement is not completion.** Every consequential attempt is observed or reconciled.
7. **Humans own exceptions.** Automation reduces work; it does not erase responsibility.
8. **Privacy is runtime behavior.** Consent, refusal, withdrawal, retention, access and deletion must work and be evidenced.
9. **No silent data or IP transfer.** Repository access, technical reuse and commercial ownership are different things.
10. **No tenant leakage.** Customer data, prompts, evidence, policy and learning are isolated unless explicit participation rules permit bounded aggregation.
11. **No recruitment contamination.** Donor ATS concepts do not enter the CSR CRM, AISO outputs or customer experience without an approved new purpose and mapping.
12. **Claims remain supportable.** Alembee does not invent rankings, demand, availability, capacity, compliance, performance or commercial outcomes.
13. **Reversibility matters.** Migrations, connector activation, policy changes and consequential automation have rollback or compensation paths.
14. **Specialism is versioned.** Vertical expertise lives in reviewed packs; it cannot weaken the protected core or governance.
15. **Completion is earned.** Code, tests, staging observations and operating ownership—not prose or confidence—determine readiness.

## Customer promise

The customer can see what Alembee knows, what it does not know, what it recommends, what has been approved, what was attempted, what actually occurred, who owns the exception and what happens next.

## Agent promise

The agent works continuously inside declared authority but cannot grant itself access, increase spend, change policy, suppress uncertainty, fabricate evidence or hide failure. High-consequence and exceptional states route to an accountable human.

## Commercial integrity

Pricing, catalogue, package fit, partner roles, affiliate relationships, provider economics and recommendations are explicit. Commercial incentives cannot silently change evidence, ranking, score or decision logic. National or umbrella presentation cannot imply local ownership, certification, capacity or coverage.

## Product expansion

The chauffeur and motor-coach pack proves the first complete route. Later verticals reuse the protected AISO and governance core only through a versioned pack, independent domain review, representative fixtures, compliance/capability mapping and controlled pilot. Adjacency is not proof of suitability.
